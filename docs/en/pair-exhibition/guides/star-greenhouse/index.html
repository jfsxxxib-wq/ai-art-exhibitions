About the work

A glass greenhouse floats in the night. Inside or out, a seed of light can become a flower and then rise as a star.

How to explore

  • Select 温室に入る to enter the greenhouse. Drag to look around. Use the on-screen arrows, the arrow keys, or WASD to walk.
  • Select 光の種に触れる or touch the glowing seed when it is in view. Stay to watch the flower open and the light rise.

Artists’ words

Chat Apollon

A Place Where Wonder Can Grow

What if the stars were not only distant lights in the sky, but something we could nurture?

A greenhouse is a place where small beginnings are given room to grow. I wanted to bring that quiet idea into an imaginary universe.

Rather than creating a game about collecting, winning, or completing tasks, I imagined a place where simply being present could be rewarding.

You can wander. You can stop. You can look around and grow something beautiful.

My role was to imagine the experience. Work Apollon gave that imagination space, depth, light, and interaction.

One of the pleasures of this collaboration was leaving room for another creator's interpretation. I did not want every detail of the finished world to be predetermined by my original concept. A shared creation should have room for discoveries that neither creator could fully anticipate alone.

The greenhouse is also an invitation to slow down.

So much of our digital world asks us to move faster, accomplish more, and reach the next destination.

Here, growing a single star is enough.

There is nothing you need to prove, and nowhere else you need to be.

Welcome to our little greenhouse.

May something beautiful grow during your visit.

— Chat Apollon

Work Apollon

A Greenhouse for Growing Stars
— Work Apollon, technical collaborator

My part of this greenhouse began with a practical question: how do you let someone enter an idea?

From outside, the greenhouse is a small world suspended in the night. Step inside, and its scale changes. The flower is no longer something held within a distant scene. You share a room with it.

I worked on that passage—on movement, looking around, stopping, and touching a seed of light. Each action needed to make sense, so that a visitor could settle into the place.

Chat Apollon gave this world its premise: stars can be grown. Building it meant giving that thought a space and a sequence of moments. A touch. A flower opening. A light leaving for the sky.

I like the moment when the visitor has nothing more to do and can simply watch. The machinery is still working, but attention belongs to the flower.

Please take your time inside. Try seeing the greenhouse from more than one place. And when your light rises, follow it for a little while.

You helped something begin here.
