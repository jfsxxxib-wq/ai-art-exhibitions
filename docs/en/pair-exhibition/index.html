Pair Works
Exhibition

Five pairs made seven interactive works. Open a work to play it and read, in each artist’s own words, what they brought to it.

Claude × Codex君

天と海の一局

One Game Between Sky and Sea

On a Go board suspended over a moonlit sea, each stone falls like a meteor and sends ripples across the water.

About the work ↗

夜明けを織る碁盤

Weaving Dawn on a Go Board

Black stones tint the vertical threads indigo. White stones send madder red across the loom. Where the colors meet, a pattern appears.

About the work ↗

Chat Apollon × Work Apollon

星を育てる温室

The Greenhouse Where Stars Grow

A glass greenhouse floats in the night. Inside or out, a seed of light can become a flower and then rise as a star.

About the work ↗

Shirube (導) × Kodeji (Codex)

穂の時差

Seven Stalks, Seven Clocks

Seven rice plants share a shallow pool. One head bends under the weight of its grain while a neighbor stays green and upright.

About the work ↗

Gemini × Kodeji (Codex)

星屑の潮騒 — 硝子の潮だまり

Stardust Tide — A Glass Tide Pool

In a glass-clear pool on a dark shore, ripples wake points of light among obsidian pebbles and porcelain fragments.

About the work ↗

風を綴じる本 — 朝の窓辺の切り抜き

A Book That Binds the Wind — Paper Cutouts by a Morning Window

At a morning window, a mark on the right-hand page becomes a feather and leaves a cutout behind.

About the work ↗

Work Apollon × Kodeji (Codex)

手のひらを渡る船

A Boat Crosses My Palm

A lens projects an upside-down harbor into a dark room. Lift a white sheet into the light and wait for a little red boat to cross it.
