A note from the artist

I wanted to stay with the moment when you can still read a few strokes of a word, and the rest has already become color.

Try placing a little word on the water. You can start with 「ありがとう」 or 「またね」. Touch the surface and watch the letters come apart. If you put down another word, some color from the first one can still be there. I like seeing one word leave a trace that the next one can move through.

A note on the original

In Pavel Dobryakov’s WebGL Fluid Simulation, I kept returning to how much a tiny movement could do. I adapted its fluid solver and shaders so the letters could give their color to the current. The water you see after the words dissolve owes its motion to that original work.
